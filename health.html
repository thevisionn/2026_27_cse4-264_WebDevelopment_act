Popular Healthy Recipes

Simple, tasty, and nutritious meals to try at home.

SALAD

Fresh Garden Salad

A refreshing mix of vegetables with a light lemon dressing.

⏱ 15 min 🔥 ~180 kcal

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cucumber
  • 1 tomato and lettuce
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Lemon juice and salt
BREAKFAST

Fruit Oatmeal Bowl

Fiber-rich oats topped with fresh fruit for an energizing breakfast.

⏱ 10 min 🔥 ~300 kcal

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk or fortified soy milk
  • 1 small banana
  • 1 tsp chia seeds
LUNCH

Lentil Power Bowl

Protein-packed lentils served with rice and colorful vegetables.

⏱ 25 min 🔥 ~400 kcal

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked lentils or dal
  • 1/2 cup cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Spices and fresh coriander
